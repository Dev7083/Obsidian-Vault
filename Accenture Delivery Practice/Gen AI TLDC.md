Data Migration Messages 
Data migration refers to the process of updating or transforming traditional data infrastructure, systems, and processes by leveraging modern technologies, architectures, and practices. This transformation often involves migrating data from legacy systems to modern platforms, ensuring compliance with evolving data governance standards, improved data quality and accessibility, all this while enabling advanced analytics and insights. 

Data Modernization Messages 
Data modernization involves updating and transforming an organization's data infrastructure, technologies, and processes to better support current and future business needs. This is especially crucial for organizations for several reasons, each of which can significantly impact their overall performance, competitive positioning, and long-term success.
