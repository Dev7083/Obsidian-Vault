
### ✅ **1. What is HealthDesk?**

HealthDesk is a complete health management web platform designed to help users manage their healthcare activities in one place. It allows users to track diseases, manage medications, plan diets, monitor fitness, find hospitals, and use AI-powered assistance for medical guidance. The goal of HealthDesk is to simplify healthcare access using digital technology.

---
### ✅ **2. What is the main objective?**

To provide a centralized digital system for managing personal healthcare efficiently and securely.

---
### ✅ **3. Key Features?**

- Disease Management
- Preventive Healthcare
- Expiry Tracker
- Yoga & Fitness
- Diet Planner
- Health Tools
- AI Assistant
- Hospital Finder

---
### ✅ **4. Role of AI Assistant?**

It helps with symptom analysis, appointment guidance, and medical queries using natural language interaction.

---
### ✅ **5. How does Hospital Finder work?**

It finds nearby hospitals using location, specialization, and user reviews.

---
### ✅ **6. Technology Used?**

Frontend: React/Next.js  
Backend: Node.js  
Database: MongoDB/Firebase  
Hosting: Vercel  
AI: Gemini-based chatbot

---
### ✅ **7. Advantages?**

- Easy health tracking
- Saves time
- User-friendly
- AI support
- Centralized data    

---
### ✅ **8. Limitations?**

- Requires internet
- AI is not a doctor
- Limited real-time diagnosis

---
### ✅ **9. Future Scope?**

- Video consultations
- Wearable integration
- Multi-language support
- Emergency services

---
### ✅ **10. Project Summary?**

HealthDesk is a smart healthcare management platform that integrates disease tracking, fitness planning, diet management, hospital search, and AI assistance into one system. It improves healthcare accessibility, reduces manual effort, and promotes preventive care through digital technology.
